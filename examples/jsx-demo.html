JSX Custom Column Demo

This demo shows JSX-style declarative rendering for custom columns with new style support.
New styles: margin, minWidth/maxWidth, minHeight/maxHeight, alignSelf, overflow, boxShadow, borderRadius per corner, border per side, textAlign, textDecoration, letterSpacing, backgroundGradient
Columns: Name | Status | Progress | Priority | Status Card | Tag | Shadow Tag | Gradient
